GDPR Compliance Statement

Last updated: April 2026

1. Our Commitment

Viper AI is committed to full compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. As a workforce compliance vetting platform that processes sensitive personal data, we recognise the critical importance of data protection and privacy.

2. Data Protection Principles

We adhere to the seven principles of UK GDPR in all our data processing activities:

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency: We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner. Candidates are informed about how their data will be used.
  • Purpose limitation: Data is collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes — workforce compliance vetting — and not processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.
  • Data minimisation: We only collect and process personal data that is necessary for compliance vetting. We do not request or store unnecessary information.
  • Accuracy: We take reasonable steps to ensure personal data is accurate and kept up to date. Our continuous monitoring features help maintain data accuracy.
  • Storage limitation: Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil its purpose, subject to statutory retention requirements.
  • Integrity and confidentiality: We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect personal data.
  • Accountability: We maintain records of processing activities and can demonstrate compliance with UK GDPR at all times.

3. Roles & Responsibilities

Role Party Responsibilities
Data Controller Staffing Agency Determines the purpose and means of processing candidate data; ensures lawful basis; informs data subjects
Data Processor Viper AI Processes data on behalf of the agency; implements security measures; maintains processing records; reports breaches
Data Subject Candidate Individual whose data is processed; has rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection

4. Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Article 6(1)(b) — Contractual necessity: Processing necessary to perform the vetting service contracted by the agency
  • Article 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation: Compliance with employment law, immigration law, and sector-specific regulations
  • Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests: Fraud prevention, service security, and improvement
  • Article 9(2)(b) — Employment obligations: Processing of special category data (health, criminal records) as required for employment compliance

5. Data Subject Rights

We support all data subject rights under UK GDPR:

5.1 Right of Access (Article 15)

Candidates can request a copy of all personal data we hold about them. This can be done through the GDPR Privacy Panel in the candidate portal, which provides an instant data export in JSON format.

5.2 Right to Rectification (Article 16)

Candidates can request correction of inaccurate personal data. Agencies can update candidate records through the platform.

5.3 Right to Erasure (Article 17)

Candidates can request deletion of their personal data through the GDPR Privacy Panel. Erasure requests are processed within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements (e.g. DBS data retention periods, employment records).

5.4 Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)

Data subjects can request restriction of processing while accuracy is being verified or where processing is unlawful.

5.5 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

Candidates can download their data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (JSON) through the candidate portal.

5.6 Right to Object (Article 21)

Data subjects can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will cease processing unless there are compelling legitimate grounds.

6. Data Protection Impact Assessments

Given the sensitive nature of compliance vetting data, we conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for:

  • AI-powered CV analysis and reference sentiment scoring
  • Continuous monitoring and automated alerting
  • Cross-candidate fraud detection
  • New feature development involving personal data processing

7. Data Breach Procedures

In the event of a personal data breach, we will:

  • Notify the ICO within 72 hours where the breach is likely to result in a risk to data subjects' rights and freedoms
  • Notify affected data controllers (agencies) without undue delay
  • Notify affected data subjects where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms
  • Document the breach, its effects, and remedial actions taken

8. International Data Transfers

Data is primarily stored and processed within the UK. Where international transfers occur (e.g. AI processing services), we ensure compliance through:

  • UK adequacy decisions
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the ICO
  • Transfer Impact Assessments where required

9. Technical & Organisational Measures

We implement the following measures to protect personal data:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for database storage
  • Role-based access controls with least-privilege principles
  • Comprehensive audit logging of all data access and modifications
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability scanning
  • Incident response and business continuity plans
  • Staff training on data protection requirements

10. Sub-processors

We use the following sub-processors to deliver the Service:

  • Railway — Application and database hosting (UK/EU)
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DNS, and DDoS protection (global with UK PoPs)
  • OpenAI — AI processing for CV analysis and reference verification (data not used for training)
  • Stripe — Payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)
  • SendGrid — Transactional email delivery

We maintain an up-to-date list of sub-processors and notify data controllers of any changes with 30 days' notice.

11. Record of Processing Activities

In accordance with Article 30, we maintain detailed records of all processing activities, including:

  • Categories of data subjects and personal data
  • Purposes of processing
  • Categories of recipients
  • Retention periods
  • Security measures in place
