Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, provide analytics, and remember your preferences. This policy explains how Viper AI uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and platform.

2. How We Use Cookies

Viper AI uses cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Service to function and cannot be disabled.

Cookie Purpose Duration
viper_refresh_token Authentication — keeps you securely logged in 7 days
viper_csrf Security — prevents cross-site request forgery attacks Session

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences to provide a better experience.

Cookie Purpose Duration
viper_tab_state Remembers your last viewed dashboard tab 30 days

2.3 Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. These help us improve the Service.

Cookie Purpose Duration
cf_analytics Cloudflare Web Analytics — privacy-first, cookieless page view analytics N/A (cookieless)

3. Third-Party Cookies

We minimise the use of third-party cookies. Our platform integrates with the following services that may set cookies:

  • Stripe: Payment processing — sets cookies necessary for secure payment handling
  • Cloudflare: Security and performance — may set cookies for bot protection

We do not use advertising cookies or allow third-party advertising networks on our Service.

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent the Service from functioning correctly.

For instructions on managing cookies in your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser local storage to store:

  • Authentication tokens (access tokens) for secure API communication
  • User preferences and dashboard state

Local storage data remains on your device until you clear your browser data or log out.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
