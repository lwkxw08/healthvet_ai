Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Viper AI ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our workforce compliance vetting platform and related services (the "Service").

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data Controller

Viper AI is the data controller for personal data processed through the Service. For agency-submitted candidate data, the staffing agency acts as the data controller and Viper AI acts as a data processor.

Contact: enquiries@viperai.io

3. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: Full name, date of birth, National Insurance number, photograph
  • Contact data: Email address, phone number, postal address
  • Employment data: CV/resume, employment history, professional qualifications, registration numbers (NMC, GMC, HCPC, CSCS, etc.)
  • Right to Work data: Passport details, visa information, share codes
  • DBS data: DBS certificate numbers, check results (we do not store criminal record details)
  • Reference data: Referee names, contact details, and responses
  • Technical data: IP addresses, browser type, device information, access logs
  • Agency/account data: Business name, billing details, user credentials

4. Special Category Data

The Service may process special category data including health information and criminal records data where necessary for compliance vetting. This processing is carried out under Article 9(2)(b) UK GDPR (employment obligations) and with appropriate safeguards in place.

5. How We Use Your Data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Conducting pre-employment compliance checks (identity, right to work, DBS, professional registration)
  • AI-powered CV analysis and reference verification
  • Continuous monitoring of credential expiry and compliance status
  • Generating compliance reports and audit trails
  • Billing and invoicing
  • Service improvement and fraud detection
  • Communicating service updates and expiry notifications

6. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contractual necessity: Processing required to deliver the vetting service
  • Legal obligation: Compliance with UK employment law, immigration law, and sector-specific regulations (CQC, Ofsted, etc.)
  • Legitimate interests: Fraud prevention, service improvement, security
  • Consent: Where required for specific processing activities (e.g. marketing communications)

7. Data Sharing

We may share personal data with:

  • The recruiting agency that submitted the candidate for vetting
  • DBS checking service providers
  • Professional registration bodies (NMC, GMC, HCPC, etc.) for verification
  • HMRC and Home Office for right-to-work verification
  • AI processing providers (OpenAI) for CV and reference analysis — data is not used for model training
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (Railway, Cloudflare) for hosting
  • Payment processors (Stripe) for billing

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

8. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for the duration required by applicable regulations:

  • Candidate vetting records: 6 years from the date of last vetting activity
  • DBS certificate data: Destroyed within 6 months of the recruitment decision (per DBS Code of Practice)
  • Account data: Duration of the account plus 2 years
  • Audit logs: 7 years

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Subject Access Request)
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing

Candidates can exercise these rights through the GDPR Privacy Panel in their candidate portal, or by contacting enquiries@viperai.io.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Audit logging of all data access
  • Regular security assessments
  • Incident response procedures

11. International Transfers

Data is primarily stored within the UK/EEA. Where data is transferred outside the UK (e.g. to AI processing providers), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to registered users. The latest version is always available on this page.

13. Contact & Complaints

For privacy enquiries: enquiries@viperai.io

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint
